Privacy Policy

App: Battery Monitorer

Effective date: 2026-04-29

This Privacy Policy explains how Battery Monitorer ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Process

The app may process the following data on your device:

  • Battery level, charging status, temperature, and timestamps.
  • Battery-related usage indicators such as screen-on state and signal strength.
  • User settings you configure in the app (alerts, theme, retention period, etc.).
  • Exported files you explicitly generate (CSV, JSON, PDF).

2. How Data Is Used

  • To show battery analytics, trends, insights, and alerts.
  • To apply your app preferences and notification settings.
  • To allow user-initiated export and sharing of battery history files.
  • To improve stability through crash and diagnostic monitoring.

3. Permissions

The app may request these Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to send battery alerts you enable.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE: to support signal-related battery insights where available.

If you deny permissions, core analytics may still work but some features may be limited.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Battery history is stored locally on your device database.
  • You can configure automatic deletion of old history (default: 30 days).
  • Export files are generated only when you request export and are stored temporarily before sharing.

5. Firebase Services

The app uses Firebase services (such as Crashlytics and Analytics) to monitor crashes, performance, and basic diagnostic events ("breadcrumbs"). These services may process technical identifiers and app diagnostics as described in Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your local battery history with third parties unless you explicitly export/share it.
  • Crash/diagnostic telemetry may be transmitted to Firebase for app reliability purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for app data handling. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Your Choices

  • You can disable notifications in app settings or system settings.
  • You can delete app data from Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • You can configure data retention days in app settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at the same policy URL and effective date will be revised.
